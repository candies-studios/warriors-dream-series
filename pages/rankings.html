How the ranking works

WDS Ranking Score = (Win% × 0.7 + Win Method Weight × 0.3) × √(total fights). Every win adds a weight for how it was won — earlier finishes count more (KO/TKO R1 0.75 · R2 0.70 · R3 0.65, Submission R1 0.70 · R2 0.65 · R3 0.60, Decision 0.55). Only official, finalized professional MMA bouts count — amateur bouts are not ranked. Women are ranked separately. Each division shows its champion and the top 10 contenders.
