WDS Fight Night 19 brings 13 professional MMA bouts to CIDCO Exhibition Ground in Vashi, Navi Mumbai. Unbeaten light heavyweight Gaganpal Singh Dua (5-0) headlines against Iran’s Mahdi Saremi (3-1), and the co-main event is a women’s catchweight bout between Surbala Laishram (2-0) and Shalini Singh. The card matches Indian fighters with opponents from Iran, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, the Philippines and Russia, and also features WDS featherweight champion Shaheen Commando and 13-fight veteran Bishal Sahu.
