Scorecards

Fighter Rankings

Score = (Win% × 0.7 + Win Method Weight × 0.3) × √(total fights). Rankings update automatically whenever results are finalized or a weight is changed; only finalized results count.

Win Method Weights

Each win adds the weight for how and in which round it ended (round 4/5 finishes use the round-3 weight). Rows marked "to confirm" are not on the committee sheet yet.
