Indian MMA Promotion Since 2022

Warriors Dream Series (WDS) is an Indian MMA promotion built to give the country’s fighters a professional stage. Since WDS Fight Night 1 in April 2022, WDS has staged 24 MMA events: 16 Championship Series Fight Nights and 8 Rising Star events, with more than 250 bouts and over 300 athletes stepping into the cage.

Most WDS events take place in Navi Mumbai, with cards also held in Mumbai and Pune. For every MMA event in India that WDS hosts, the full fight card and results are published on this site: every bout, winner, method and round.

Two series, one path to the top

Championship Series Fight Nights are professional MMA cards, where India’s best fighters meet each other and international opponents. Rising Star events are where amateur and up-and-coming fighters get their first experience under the lights, and many of today’s Fight Night athletes started there.

Indian fighters against the world

WDS cards increasingly match Indian athletes with fighters from abroad. WDS Fight Night 19, on Saturday 12 December 2026 at CIDCO Exhibition Ground in Vashi, Navi Mumbai, features opponents from Iran, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, the Philippines and Russia, with a women’s catchweight bout as the co-main event.

Fans can follow each fighter from their first Rising Star bout to a Fight Night main event: every athlete has a profile page with their WDS record, results and full fight history, and every event has its own page with the complete card.

Behind the promotion is founder Sanjivan Padwal, a coach, promoter and former professional fighter, and the team at Fit and Fight Club in Navi Mumbai and Pune. Browse every WDS fighter’s record, look back at past fight cards and results, or read more about WDS.
